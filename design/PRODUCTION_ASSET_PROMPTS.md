# НОЛЬ V2 — production asset prompts

These prompts document the standalone raster assets derived from the approved
V2 posters. UI text, controls, labels and brand marks are never baked into the
assets.

## Shared direction

Museum-grade contemporary editorial photography, restrained monochrome
palette, physically plausible light, deep negative space, subtle material
grain, no advertising smiles, no logos, no readable personal data, no text,
no UI, no red areas, no neon, no generic corporate imagery.

## Hero portal

Dark architectural void above a softly reflective black plane; a large
off-centre circular environment made primarily from suspended white light,
fine particles and haze; the visible geometry is subordinate to light; soft
horizon and restrained reflection; no metallic torus, no exaggerated bloom.

Outputs:

- desktop `16:10`, portal right of the semantic centre;
- mobile portrait, opening above and behind the future text-safe area.

## Problem documentary room

Night interior observed as documentary evidence; one person at a table with
papers and phones, no readable data; one restrained phone reflection; a dark
wall and table provide clean projection fields; quiet, non-commercial realism.

Output: desktop `16:10`, composition safe for a right-wall HTML projection.

## Brand fold

Monumental black material plane physically peeling back to reveal warm white
paper/light; a small human figure at the fold; central threshold shaped by the
opening rather than an added ring; reflective floor; no text.

Output: wide `16:9`.

## Process thresholds

Five deep architectural thresholds in one dark continuous field; the central
threshold receives soft white light; adjacent thresholds remain coordinates;
enough clear lower space for an HTML route; no labels, numbers or red line.

Output: wide `16:9`.

## Media studio

Empty black leather chair in a dark documentary studio; focused overhead
projector beam; tactile wall and floor; frontal composition; generous wall
space for HTML title projection; no camera UI, no title, no typography.

Output: wide `16:9`.

## Media contact strip

One vertical analogue film strip containing exactly three monochrome frames:
an empty chair by a window, an open notebook with a pen, and a long dark
corridor; real perforations and restrained wear; no numbers, captions or text.

Output: tall portrait strip.

## Final threshold

View from a rough dark cave through a naturally circular opening into calm warm
dawn; restrained modern architecture, shallow reflections, one distant person
walking forward; the opening itself is the Zero portal; no added glowing ring,
no victory pose, no text.

Outputs:

- wide `16:9`;
- independently composed portrait crop retaining the full opening and person.

## Negative prompt for all outputs

Typography, captions, logos, brand names, legal documents with readable data,
watermarks, stock-photo handshakes, lawyers in suits, judge gavel, scales,
money, luxury cars, celebratory people, red glow, cyberpunk neon, heavy bloom,
obvious metal torus, over-sharpening, UI panels, cards, buttons.
